Bull Market vs Bear Market

2026-09-10

A bull market is a sustained stretch of generally rising prices and optimism; a bear market is a prolonged period of falling prices and caution.

The labels describe the broad mood and direction over time, not a precise threshold, and different people date them differently.

Crypto tends to swing between these phases more sharply than many markets. The terms are useful shorthand for context, not a prediction of what comes next.
