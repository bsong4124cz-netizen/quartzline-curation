“Support” That Messages You First Is a Scam

2026-09-10

A common trick is an impostor posing as customer support who reaches out first — in a DM, a reply, or a chat group — offering to “help” with a problem you mentioned.

Real support does not slide into your direct messages, and it never needs remote control of your screen, your passwords, or your recovery phrase.

If you need help, start from the official app or website yourself. Anyone who contacts you first claiming to be support should be treated as a scammer until proven otherwise.
