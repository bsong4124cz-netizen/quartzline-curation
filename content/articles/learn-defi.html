What “DeFi” Means

2026-09-10

DeFi, or decentralised finance, refers to financial services — trading, lending, borrowing — built from smart contracts rather than run by a company.

Instead of an account at an institution, you interact directly from your own wallet, and the rules are enforced by code on a public network.

The appeal is openness and self-custody; the trade-off is that you carry the risk yourself, from contract bugs to your own mistakes, with no support desk to call.
