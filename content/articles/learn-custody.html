Custodial vs Non-Custodial Wallets

2026-09-10

In a custodial wallet, a company holds the keys on your behalf — much like a bank. In a non-custodial (self-custody) wallet, you hold the keys yourself.

Custody is convenient and lets someone help you recover access, but it means trusting that company. Self-custody removes that dependency but puts full responsibility on you.

The old phrase “not your keys, not your coins” captures the trade-off: control and responsibility come together.
