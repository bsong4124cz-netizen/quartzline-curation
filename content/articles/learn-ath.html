What an All-Time High (ATH) Is

2026-09-10

An all-time high is the highest price an asset has ever reached. You will also see all-time low (ATL) for the opposite.

ATHs are widely watched as psychological markers, but a price being near or far from its ATH tells you about history, not about what happens next.

Because different data sources start at different times and use different markets, reported ATHs can vary slightly between apps.
