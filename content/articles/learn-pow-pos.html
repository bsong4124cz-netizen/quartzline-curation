Proof of Work vs Proof of Stake

2026-09-10

Both are ways a decentralised network agrees on which transactions are valid. Proof of Work has computers compete to solve hard puzzles, spending energy to earn the right to add a block — the model Bitcoin uses.

Proof of Stake instead has participants lock up coins as collateral and be chosen to add blocks, with misbehaviour penalised by losing that stake. Ethereum moved to this model.

The trade-offs are debated endlessly, mostly around energy use, security and decentralisation. For a general reader, the main point is that they are two different answers to the same problem.
