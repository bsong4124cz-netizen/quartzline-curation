Candlestick vs Line Charts

2026-09-10

A line chart connects closing prices into a single line — clean and good for seeing the overall trend at a glance.

A candlestick chart shows more per period: each “candle” marks the open, close, high and low, so you can see the range and whether the period finished up or down.

Neither is “better”. Lines are easier to scan; candles carry more detail. Pick whichever answers the question you are asking.
