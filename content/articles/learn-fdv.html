What Fully Diluted Valuation (FDV) Is

2026-09-10

Fully diluted valuation estimates what a coin's market cap would be if every coin that will ever exist were already in circulation — price multiplied by the maximum or total supply.

It matters when a large share of supply has not yet been released: a coin can look small by market cap but much larger by FDV, hinting at future supply pressure.

Comparing market cap and FDV side by side is a quick way to see how much a project's valuation depends on coins that are not yet on the market.
