Rug Pulls: What They Are and the Warning Signs

2026-09-10

A rug pull is when the people behind a token drain its liquidity or dump their holdings, collapsing the price and leaving holders with something they cannot sell.

Common red flags include an anonymous team with no track record, a huge share of supply held by a few wallets, and heavy marketing that leans on hype and urgency.

New, thinly traded tokens carry this risk by nature. Understanding who controls the supply and the liquidity tells you more than the price chart does.
