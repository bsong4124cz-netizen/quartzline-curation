Bitcoin Dominance, Explained

2026-09-10

Bitcoin dominance is Bitcoin's share of the entire crypto market's capitalisation. If the whole market is worth $2.5T and Bitcoin is $1.4T, dominance is about 56%.

Watchers use it as a rough sentiment gauge. Rising dominance often means money is concentrating in Bitcoin; falling dominance can indicate more interest flowing into other coins.

It is a ratio, so it can change because Bitcoin moved, because other coins moved, or both. Read it as context, not a signal on its own.
