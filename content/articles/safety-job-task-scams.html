Fake Job and “Task” Scams

2026-09-10

These start as an easy remote job — rating apps, “boosting” listings, completing tasks — that pays small amounts at first, then asks you to deposit crypto to unlock higher earnings.

The early payouts are bait. Once you deposit to “continue”, the money is gone and the tasks never end.

A legitimate job never requires you to put your own crypto in to get paid. Any “work” that asks you to deposit first is a scam.
